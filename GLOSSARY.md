# PJ's Game

A tower defense game about nature fighting back against tech. Robots march along a windy path toward your Base, and towers shaped like animals and mystical creatures stop them with elemental attacks.

## Robots

**Robot**:
A mechanical invader that walks the path toward the Base. Robots are the bad guys.
_Avoid_: Monster, enemy, bad guy

**Clanker**:
The weakest Robot: a wobbly yellow farm bot on four pairs of wheels, hobbling along. The first Robot you meet.
_Avoid_: Jank bot, farm bot

**Scout**:
A small, fast Robot. Shows up after the Clanker.

**Walker**:
A regular Robot with average speed and health.

**Tank**:
A slow Robot with lots of health.

**Boss**:
A huge Robot that arrives on Waves 5, 10 and 15.

**Hider**:
A Robot that turns invisible for 3 seconds every time it gets hit. While hidden it can't be damaged, but it keeps walking toward the Base.

**Aura**:
A colored glow around a Robot that shows it's stronger and has more health than a normal one of its kind. From weakest to strongest: no Aura, blue, purple, orange. Stronger Auras show up in later Waves and on harder Difficulties.

## Towers

**Tower**:
An animal or mystical creature you place on a grass square to stop Robots. Towers are the good guys and can't be hurt.

**Element**:
The kind of attack a Tower uses. Fire burns, Water rusts Robots so they slow down, Plant vines hold Robots in place, Air pushes Robots back.

**Helper**:
A Tower that doesn't attack. It makes nearby Towers stronger. The Wizard is a Helper and makes nearby Towers attack faster.

**Healer**:
A glowing deer Tower that gives back 1 Base Health after every Wave, more when upgraded, and turns Factory squares back into grass. Healers are expensive, even the first one. There's no limit on how many you can have, and their healing adds up.

**Vision**:
The power to see and hit Hiders while they're invisible. Owls have Vision; other Towers can get it from an upgrade.

**Upgrade**:
Spending Maple Leaves to make a Tower stronger, up to 3 times. Each Upgrade changes how the Tower looks: the tree giant starts as a small sapling and grows, and the owl only gets its glowing aura at the top level. A Tower can also be sold back for half price.

## The map

**Base**:
Your home at the end of the path. The Robots are trying to reach it.
_Avoid_: Home

**Base Health**:
How much life the Base has left; it starts at 100. A Robot that reaches the Base takes away as much Base Health as the Robot had left.

**Factory**:
Land the Robots have turned into machinery. Every 1 Base Health lost turns one of 100 squares into Factory, starting from the Robots' side of the map and spreading toward the Base. When all 100 are Factory, you lose. Towers standing on Factory do 2 less damage.

## Playing

**Score**:
Points for every Robot destroyed (bigger Robots are worth more), plus a bonus for the Base Health left, multiplied by the Difficulty (Nightmare ×3).

**Leaderboard**:
A list of the best Scores, by nickname. Each Level has an all-time Leaderboard, and each Challenge has its own.

**Challenge**:
A short code (like LEAF-42) that friends type in to play the same Level on the same Difficulty, each on their own device. The person who makes it picks how long it lasts (1 hour, 1 day or 1 week; 1 day to start), and it has its own Leaderboard.

**Nickname**:
The name a player shows on a Leaderboard. There are no logins or emails.

**Wave**:
One group of Robots. You press Start Wave when you're ready for the next one.
_Avoid_: Round, turn

**Level**:
One map, played through 15 Waves. Level 1 is the Meadow, which has a long, twisty path, and that makes it easier. Level 2 is the Forest, which has a straighter path, and that makes it harder. Beating a Level unlocks the next.
_Avoid_: Map, stage

**Maple Leaf**:
The money of the game. A destroyed Robot drops Maple Leaves that fly quickly up to your money bar. Towers and Upgrades cost Maple Leaves.
_Avoid_: Acorn, coin

**Difficulty**:
How tough the Robots are: Easy, Normal, Hard or Nightmare. Harder means faster and stronger Robots. Only on Easy does the Base heal a little on its own after each Wave.

**Stars**:
What you earn for beating a Level: 1 to 3, depending on how much Base Health you have left.

**Tutorial**:
Optional guidance you can turn on when you start a game. It walks you through the first 5 Waves with tips, then goes away.
_Avoid_: Hints

**Save Slot**:
One of three places a game in progress is kept. You pick a Save Slot to start or continue a game.
